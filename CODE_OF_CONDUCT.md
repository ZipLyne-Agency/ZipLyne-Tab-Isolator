# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a constructive, technically rigorous place to collaborate.

Be respectful, assume good intent, criticize ideas rather than people, and keep reports focused on reproducible behavior. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. If a situation involves immediate safety or abuse, use GitHub's platform reporting tools. Do not publish private information while reporting conduct concerns.
